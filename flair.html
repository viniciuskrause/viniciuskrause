Flair Salesforce HR SaaS

Timeline
6-month product development
Team
Product Design, Engineering, Product Management

Overview

Flair is a Salesforce-based HR platform designed to simplify employee and administrative workflows. Built from the ground up, the project focused on creating a modern, scalable experience while leveraging Salesforce’s component ecosystem.

The challenge

Designing a product that felt intuitive and polished while working within Salesforce’s technical constraints. The goal was to balance usability, consistency, and implementation efficiency without relying on excessive customization.

My role

I led the product design from concept to implementation, defining the UX strategy, visual language, and reusable component library. I also partnered closely with engineers throughout development to ensure the final product matched the design vision.

Solution

By building a consistent component-based interface aligned with Salesforce’s architecture, we delivered an HR platform that was modern, scalable, and straightforward to implement and maintain.
